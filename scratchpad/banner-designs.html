Win Banner — Design Scratchpad

Five candidate directions for the "recent wins" carousel. Mock data only. Rotates every 4s; arrows/dots manual.

Design 1 — Cinematic banner

Full-width hero. Champion splash as background, heavy left veil, big type. Best for a single rotating "latest win" spotlight.

Design 2 — Card row carousel

Several wins visible at once, poster-style cards. Good middle ground: shows history, not just latest.

Design 3 — Ticker strip

Thin strip that slides wins horizontally. Lowest visual weight; could sit directly under the header without competing with the grid.
